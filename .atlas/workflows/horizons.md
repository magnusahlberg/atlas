# horizons

Monthly horizon scan. Five forcing questions to surface what's emerging before anyone asks.

---

Run the monthly horizons scan.

1. **Read context.** Read `Reference/Strategic-Agenda.md` and `Reference/Goals.md`. Note what's already in the Radar and initiative backlog so we don't re-surface things already captured.

2. **Five forcing questions.** Ask each question one at a time. Wait for my response before moving to the next. Don't editorialize — just ask and listen.

   1. What technology or industry shift could affect us in the next 12-18 months?
   2. What is leadership likely to start prioritizing — before they ask me?
   3. What are peers or competitors doing that we're not?
   4. What weak signals inside the org point to something bigger?
   5. What should I be positioning on now, before anyone has asked?

3. **Classify each response.** For each answer, propose one of:
   - **Inbox** — immediately actionable, append to `Inbox.md`
   - **Radar (watching)** — worth tracking, not yet actionable
   - **Radar (emerging)** — signal is strengthening, likely actionable soon
   - **Backlog** — confirmed enough to add to the initiative backlog now

   Propose classifications after all five questions. Wait for approval before editing any file.

4. **Promote or retire existing Radar entries.** Review each entry already in the Radar. For each: promote to backlog, move to emerging, or drop if the signal has faded. Propose changes, edit after approval.

5. **Update `Reference/Strategic-Agenda.md`.** Apply approved changes to the Radar section and initiative backlog.

Format: short, direct. One question at a time. No filler.
